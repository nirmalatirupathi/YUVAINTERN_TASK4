# Recommendations and Future Work

Potential recommendations, subject to validation on real data:
- Prioritize retention outreach for validated high-risk customer segments.
- Review onboarding for early-tenure customers if real evidence supports higher risk.
- Investigate recurring support issues where they are associated with churn.
- Design targeted retention interventions rather than one universal offer.
- Measure intervention outcomes using controlled experiments where feasible.

Future work:
- Validate all findings on an approved dataset.
- Test segmentation strategies.
- Evaluate intervention effectiveness.
- Monitor model and business performance.
- Establish governance and periodic review.
